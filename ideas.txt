#########################################################################
Wizard rogue-like game with a focus on spell casting in combat.
Killing enemies will drop items that can be sacrificed to gain new spells.
These spells can be used in combat, and can also be combined with other spells to create new effects.
#########################################################################
A portal will randomly spawn in each game that will take the player to a new harder area.
The player will fight a boss at the end of each area.
